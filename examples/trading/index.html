Learn a trading strategy with PPO

Watch an agent learn from a synthetic market, then inspect its decisions on a separate evaluation series.

Educational simulation only. Synthetic prices and simplified execution do not represent real markets. This is not financial advice.

1. Train the agent

PPO collects experience from the training market and updates a policy that chooses a position. Training runs in a worker so the page stays responsive.

At each step the agent observes the latest return, trailing three- and five-step returns, and its current position. Its continuous PPO action becomes short, flat, or long. The reward is the position's next log return minus a transaction cost when the position changes.

Ready. Run zero iterations to inspect the untrained policy.

2. Inspect the held-out evaluation

The policy sees only information available at each decision. The evaluation uses a different synthetic series for 64 steps. Price starts at 100 and equity at 1; each position applies to the interval ending at that point. The chart lines have separate scales. Positions are long (+1), flat (0), or short (-1).

Changing position costs 0.05% per unit of position change (a direct long-to-short flip costs 0.10%). Equity is marked to market, with no forced closing cost at the end. Market impact, financing, taxes, and bracket orders are not modeled.

3. What to look for

Compare the same seed before and after training. A profitable simulated run does not guarantee a profitable real strategy: costs, market changes, and random variation matter. Try several seeds instead of relying on one result.
